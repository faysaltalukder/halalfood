How to read the figures

Net cash movement

Confirmed money in minus confirmed money out. It is not business profit.

Account balance

Opening balance plus confirmed movements after the account opening date.

Gross profit

Not displayed as a reliable figure until net sales and cost of goods sold are both complete.

COD variance

Used to investigate settlement differences only. It is not automatically a loss or receivable.
