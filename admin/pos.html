Offline Sales Import

Bring handwritten-counter sales, shop sales or sales recorded outside this website into your central business records.

No file selected yet.

Payment handlingEach sale uses the payment_received CSV field. “yes” creates a Confirmed income entry; “no” keeps it as Draft. This records the supplied information and is not bank verification.
CSV columns

sale_reference, sale_date, customer_name, customer_phone, customer_email, payment_method, payment_received, notes, product_slug, quantity, unit_price.

  • Use the same sale_reference on every product row belonging to one receipt. A different reference starts a separate sale.
  • sale_date must be YYYY-MM-DD. Customer name and phone can be blank for walk-in customers.
  • Payment method: cash, mobile_banking, bank_transfer, cash_on_delivery or other. Blank defaults to cash.
  • payment_received: yes/no. Blank defaults to yes. unit_price is optional; blank uses the current catalogue price.
  • Use the exact product_slug shown in Product Mapping. The same file can be imported again safely: already-imported sale references are skipped.

Select a CSV to validate it. No records are saved until you review the preview and choose Import.
