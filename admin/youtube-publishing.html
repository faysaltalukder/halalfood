ⓘ
Why is publishing completed in Studio?The published YouTube Data API does not expose a documented Community image-post creation method. This dashboard will not claim an image post is published automatically. It validates and previews images locally, saves caption/file-name metadata only, copies the caption, and opens your channel's Posts page in YouTube Studio. YouTube may limit posts per 24 hours, and Community posts are unavailable for some channel/account states, including Made for Kids channels.
