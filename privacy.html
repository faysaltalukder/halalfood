    Privacy Policy

    This website collects information you submit through the order form, such as your name, phone number, address, quantity, and optional notes, so that we can process and confirm your order.

    How order information is used

    Submitted order information is used for order handling, delivery coordination, and customer communication. The order form is connected to a Google Apps Script and Google Sheet workflow.

    Direct contact

    If you contact us by phone or message, the information you provide may be used to respond to your request or process an order.

    Third-party services

    This website uses GitHub Pages for hosting and a Google Apps Script endpoint for order submission. Third-party services may process technical or submitted information according to their own policies.

    Questions

    For privacy questions, contact us using the phone number or contact options on this website.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How can I place an order?

    Select a product, use the order option, enter your delivery information, and submit the order. You can also contact us through WhatsApp for order assistance.

    What payment method is available?

    We accept Cash on Delivery only. Please do not send money in advance. Payment is made after you receive the products.

    Can I confirm product availability before ordering?

    Yes. You can contact us by phone, email, or WhatsApp to confirm current stock, packaging, and delivery details.

    Where do you deliver?

    Our current delivery information covers the districts listed on the website. Please confirm delivery availability for your address before finalizing an order.

    Can I cancel or change an order?

    Contact us as soon as possible with your order details. Changes depend on whether the order has already been prepared or dispatched.

    What should I do if an item is missing or damaged?

    Contact us promptly after delivery with your order details and a description of the issue so we can review it and advise you on the next step.
